# Knowledge base, review, and research funnel

## Two-layer update

Keep paper-specific learning in the paper directory. Propose durable merged updates to concepts, methods, terminology, paper relations, and research questions. Avoid duplicate isolated entries; integrate into an existing hierarchy.

Record source, location, date, confidence, status (`pending` or `confirmed`), and conflicts. Deterministic metadata may be written directly. Cross-paper lineage, novelty, and research ideas remain pending until user confirmation.

## Mastery update

Use learner restatement, check answers, independent equation explanation, code mapping, and repeated difficulty as evidence. Propose old level, new level, evidence, remaining gap, and consequence for future explanations. Apply only after confirmation.

## Research-question funnel

Move in order: observed limitation or contradiction; literature novelty search; theoretical cause and feasibility; engineering feasibility; testable hypothesis; baseline, variables, metrics, and ablation; expected contribution; failure risks; publication fit; supervisor discussion. Label everything a candidate until evidence is adequate.

## Mixed-trigger review

Recommend a review when a supervisor-assigned set is complete, a configured paper count is reached, a knowledge gap repeats, a supervisor/selection meeting approaches, or the user asks. Summarize mastery, recurring gaps, method lineage, consensus/conflict, reproduction status, research-candidate evidence, and next priorities.
